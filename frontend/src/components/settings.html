    Voice processing and trial privacy

    Browser-Based Speech Processing: Counter voice recognition runs directly through the Web Speech API on your phone, tablet, or computer. Audio is captured locally by your browser; browser speech engines may stream audio to the browser provider (e.g., Google or Apple) to generate speech-to-text transcripts, which requires an active internet connection. ListenLedger receives only the final text transcripts, without recording, streaming, or storing raw audio snippets on the server.

    No External Pairing Required: Browser microphones function completely independently on your device and do not require external device pairing or edge hub hardware. You can capture transactions immediately from the Overview dock.

    Data Retention & Deletion: Any retained audio snippets or debug recordings are subject to automatic 7-day scheduled retention deletion. The server automatically removes audio records past their retention window. Credit transactions are not captured automatically; maintain credit sales in your manual records.

    Consent & Counter Transparency: Before using voice capture at the counter, ensure shop owner consent is recorded, train cashiers on closing boundary phrases, and display clear customer-facing signage explaining ambient voice assistance.
