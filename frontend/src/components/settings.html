    Voice processing and trial privacy

    Use the microphone on this phone from Overview. External edge hardware is parked for this trial; do not connect the demonstration runner to a real shop.

    Browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser provider and normally needs an internet connection. This pilot version sends transcripts to ListenLedger, without recording or uploading audio snippets. The server discards snippets sent by older clients. Browser-provider processing is separate from ListenLedger storage.

    New raw-audio retention is disabled pending an opt-in policy and automatic deletion controls. Existing recordings and legacy device queues are not automatically erased by this change. Credit capture is parked; keep credit sales in your independent records.

    Before field recording, obtain the owner's consent, inform staff and display a customer notice. Collect P1 evidence with an ordinary phone recorder, keep recordings out of git, store them encrypted and delete them after labelling.
